Persistent App Shell

The tab bar and audio bar now stay on screen no matter how deep you navigate, so you never lose your place or your playing audio.

1

Open a verse from the Available list

From the Verses tab, tapping a verse opens its detail view. Before this change, this replaced the whole screen — the tab bar disappeared until you backed all the way out.

Verses → tap "Romans 8:28" → Verse Detail (tab bar still visible at bottom)
2

Audio keeps playing while you browse

Starting audio review on one verse and then switching to Settings no longer stops playback or hides the controls — the audio bar rides above the tab bar on every tab.

[Audio bar: Romans 8:28 ▶▮▮] — visible on Home, Verses, Review, Test, and Settings alike
3

Back button steps within a tab, not across tabs

Each tab (Home, Verses, Review, Test, Settings) keeps its own navigation history. Pressing back from Verse Detail returns to the Verses list — it never jumps to a different tab or exits the app early.

Verses → Verse Detail → [Back] → Verses (still on the Verses tab)
4

Switching tabs remembers where you were

Leaving Verse Detail to check Settings and then tapping back to Verses returns you to Verse Detail, not the top of the list — each tab preserves its own place.

Verses (Verse Detail open) → Settings → Verses → back at Verse Detail

What you just saw

  • Tab bar and audio bar are always reachable, at any navigation depth.
  • Each tab keeps its own back-navigation history, independent of the others.
  • Switching tabs and returning restores exactly where you left off.
  • Audio review is never interrupted by browsing other tabs.
