Recite Aloud (Speech-to-Text)

Speaking a memorized verse instead of typing it or self-rating — scored on-device, with the spoken words never saved anywhere.

1

Start a Recite test

From the Test tab, choose Recite format. By default you'll see the verse reference and rate yourself "I knew it" / "Didn't know" — the mic is entirely optional.

Test → Recite → Start test
2

Tap the mic to recite aloud instead

Tapping the microphone button requests the RECORD_AUDIO permission right then — not at app launch — so nothing is requested until you actually choose to speak an answer.

🎤 Listening… (tap to stop)
3

Speak the verse, pause as needed

Recognition runs entirely on-device and tolerates up to 15 seconds of silence, so pausing mid-verse to recall the next line no longer cuts you off early.

"For I know the plans I have for you… (pause) …declares the Lord."
4

See what was heard and your score

The transcript is shown once, scored the same way a typed answer would be, and never written to your test history or any log.

Heard: "For I know the plans I have for you… declares the Lord."
Try Again Continue
5

Try Again if it mis-heard you

Try Again clears the transcript and score without recording an attempt, so a recognition mistake doesn't count against you — just recite again.

Privacy note Speech recognition never leaves your device and has no cloud fallback. If on-device recognition isn't available on your phone, the mic button simply won't work — typing or self-rating are always there as the default path.

What you just saw

  • Recite mode's default is still typing or self-rating — the mic is opt-in, never required.
  • RECORD_AUDIO is requested only at the moment you tap the mic, not pre-granted at launch.
  • Recognition happens on-device, with no cloud service involved at any point.
  • A 15-second pause tolerance means a mid-verse memory gap won't end your attempt early.
  • The spoken transcript is shown once for confirmation and then discarded — never saved to history or logs.
